## Gaps (questions for the interview)

1. The spec says "maintainer", the roles document says "Contributor" (and Manager, Administrator), the brief says "sponsor / reporter", and the page says "reviewer" where the spec says "manager". The glossary has none of them. Which words do the stories use for the person who imports and the person who reviews? — Every story names its actor; the glossary must hold the term.
2. Is "Import a report" governed by the workspace permission "Import data" (default: only administrators), or is that permission about something else and import follows "can edit the project" alone? — The two written rules give opposite answers for a Contributor.
3. What does a person who can see the project but not edit it get: are the three entry points hidden, and what does the page's address show them? — Nothing in the prototype checks a role.
4. Is the import offered while the project page is in its reading state, or only after Edit is pressed? — The page has a view and an edit mode; the entry points ignore it today.
5. What can fail while reading, and what does the person see for each: a scanned or password-protected PDF, an empty file, a file over the limit, a reading that times out, the reading service being down? — The prototype has one generic "Couldn't read *name*" line.
6. A real reading can take minutes (the local reader allows four). Does the person wait on the page, or can they leave and come back to a finished reading? — Decides whether a reading is a stored thing with its own state.
7. What does the check step show when the reading finds nothing to propose, or only "Not used" lines? — No empty state exists.
8. What happens on a project with no performance measures, or no closed reporting period to file under? — The measures card hides its button with no measures; the page itself has no such state.
9. What can fail on "Add N changes" (the record changed underneath, the save fails part-way), and is an import all-or-nothing? — Validation is all-or-nothing today; saving is unspecified.
10. How long is the document kept, can it be downloaded, who can delete it, and is opening it logged? — Decision 19 says who may see it and nothing else.
11. Is the reading itself kept: the changes the person left out, the "Not used" and "Skipped" lists? — The code calls the "Not used" list "the program lead's evidence of a question they are not asking", but nothing stores or shows it after Add.
12. How long is Undo available, and to whom: only on the toast, only to the person who imported, still after other edits to the same fields, still after a publish? — Today it is gone when the toast is dismissed.
13. What are the limits: file size (20 MB shown), file types (.doc, .xls, .txt are inconsistent), pages or rows in a document, length of pasted text, length of the note? — And what the person sees at each limit.
14. When two people import the same report, or someone edits a field while another has the check step open, which value stands and is anyone told? — "Already on file" is computed when the reading opens, not when Add is pressed.
15. Does adding an import notify anyone (the lead organization's managers), or does it only raise the unpublished-changes count? — No notification is shown.
16. When a report names a funding source the record already has with a different amount, is that a change to the existing source or "Already on file"? — Today name and organization matching makes it "Already on file" and the new amount is lost.
